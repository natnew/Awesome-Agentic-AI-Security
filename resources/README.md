# Resources

This section will hold curated external resources for agentic AI security. It is not intended to become a flat list of links. Each substantial entry should explain why the resource matters, what it covers, how mature it is, and where its limitations are.

The resource catalogue should help readers distinguish standards, frameworks, vendor research, independent research, tools, benchmarks, guides, and opinion pieces.

## Planned Coverage

Future resource pages will cover:

- Standards and frameworks.
- Vendor research.
- Independent research and papers.
- Tools for securing, observing, testing, or governing agentic systems.
- Benchmarks and evaluation harnesses.

## Entry Quality

Substantial resource entries should include:

- Resource type.
- Producer or publisher.
- Relevance to agentic execution security.
- Risks, behaviours, controls, benchmarks, or architectures covered.
- Evidence quality and maturity level.
- Last-checked date.
- Important limitations or caveats.

## Selection Principle

The repository favours fewer, better-labelled resources over a large unstructured catalogue. A resource should strengthen the reader's understanding of agentic execution security, practical control design, evaluation evidence, governance, or research direction.
